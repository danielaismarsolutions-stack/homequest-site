HQ Housing Terms & Conditions Assistance in Financing and Buying a Property

At HQ Housing we are happy to assist you in finding, negotiating and purchasing your ideal property. The following terms apply to all property purchase and financing assistance services.

Costs

HQ Housing charges a brokerage fee equal to 1.25% of the agreed purchase price, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

All fees are exclusive of VAT.

The brokerage fee shall become payable in two stages.

Stage 1 – Accepted Offer

Upon acceptance by the seller of an offer submitted by or through HQ Housing, 40% of the agreed brokerage fee becomes immediately due and payable.

For the purpose of these Terms & Conditions, an offer shall be deemed accepted once the seller, selling agent or representative confirms acceptance of the Client's offer, whether verbally, in writing, by email, WhatsApp, through a memorandum of sale, or through execution of a purchase agreement.

The first installment invoice shall be payable within seven (7) calendar days after acceptance of the offer by the seller.

The first installment shall remain payable irrespective of whether the Client subsequently withdraws, delays signing, fails to complete the purchase process, or is unable to obtain financing.

Stage 2 – Financing Approval and Transfer Date

Upon:

  • obtaining financing approval;
  • receiving a binding mortgage offer;
  • obtaining lender commitment;
  • or otherwise securing the required financing;

and

  • confirmation of the transfer date, completion date or notarial transfer appointment,

the remaining 60% of the agreed brokerage fee becomes immediately due and payable, less the Activation Deposit already paid.

The remaining balance must be paid within seven (7) calendar days of invoice date and, in any event, no later than the scheduled transfer date.

Activation Deposit

Following registration for purchase assistance, HQ Housing may assist the Client in exploring the feasibility of a property purchase and may introduce the Client to independent mortgage advisers, banks, lenders or other financing specialists.

HQ Housing does not provide mortgage advice, financial advice, tax advice or legal advice.

No Activation Deposit shall be due during this preliminary assessment phase.

Once an independent mortgage adviser, bank, lender or other financing specialist has confirmed that the Client is, in principle, financially capable of acquiring a property within the intended budget range, a non-refundable Activation Deposit of €750 excluding VAT shall become immediately due and payable.

Upon payment of the Activation Deposit, the active purchase assignment shall commence.

The Activation Deposit covers, among other things:

  • onboarding and intake;
  • market analysis;
  • property search activities;
  • purchase strategy preparation;
  • arranging and coordinating viewings;
  • negotiations;
  • communication with selling agents;
  • coordination of the purchase process.

The Activation Deposit shall be deducted from the final brokerage fee upon successful completion of the transaction.

Scope of Services

HQ Housing may assist with:

  • identifying suitable properties;
  • arranging and attending viewings;
  • market analysis and purchase strategy;
  • preparing and submitting purchase offers;
  • negotiating purchase terms;
  • coordinating communication with selling agents;
  • introducing financing providers;
  • coordinating communication with mortgage advisers, banks and lenders;
  • coordinating communication with notaries and other parties involved in the transaction.

Financing Assistance

HQ Housing may introduce the Client to independent mortgage advisers, banks, private banks, lenders and alternative financing providers.

HQ Housing does not provide mortgage advice, financial advice, tax advice, legal advice or regulated financial intermediation services.

HQ Housing does not act as a mortgage adviser, lender, mortgage broker or financial institution.

The Client remains solely responsible for selecting, accepting and relying upon any financing advice, financing proposal, mortgage offer or financing solution.

HQ Housing does not guarantee the availability, approval, terms or conditions of any financing.

No Guarantee

HQ Housing does not guarantee:

  • acceptance of any offer;
  • successful negotiations;
  • financing approval;

Withdrawal

If the Client withdraws prior to acceptance of an offer, no additional brokerage fee shall be due other than the Activation Deposit already paid.

If the Client withdraws after acceptance of an offer by the seller, the first installment equal to 40% of the agreed brokerage fee shall remain fully due and payable.

De bemiddelingsvergoeding is in twee fasen verschuldigd.
