**About HQ Housing**

My name is **Philippe Kamoun**. I was born and raised in North Holland and moved to Amsterdam with my parents at the age of eleven. There, my parents opened a restaurant on the Overtoom, in a building that would later become home to Bagels & Beans. Looking back, that move marked the beginning of my lifelong involvement in real estate.

From an early age, I worked hands-on in property renovations, learning practical skills such as plumbing, electrical work and plastering. As our family property portfolio expanded, I gradually became involved in tenant screening, lettings, property management and real estate transactions.

After beginning my professional career at **PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC)**, I returned to the real estate sector, where I progressed through various roles including **property administrator, controller and asset manager**. During this period, I was involved in the administration, financial management and strategic oversight of large residential property portfolios, working with both national and international stakeholders.

Later, I founded **M&L International**, providing property management and advisory services to private and international clients. Over the years, I have gained experience across virtually every aspect of the real estate industry, including property management, acquisitions, lettings, asset management, development and real estate finance.

Today, after decades of experience in the sector, I founded **HQ Housing**. The company was created in response to the growing complexity of the Dutch housing market, where expats, international professionals, entrepreneurs and private buyers often struggle to navigate a highly competitive environment.

HQ Housing combines extensive market knowledge, a strong professional network and a highly personal approach. Whether you are looking to rent a home, purchase a property or explore financing opportunities, our objective is to guide you through the process efficiently, transparently and with genuine personal attention.

Unlike many traditional agencies, we focus on tailored solutions rather than standardised processes. Every client has a unique situation, every search has its own challenges, and every successful outcome starts with the right strategy.

At **HQ Housing**, we do not simply help people find a property. We help them secure the right home.
