What Apply Yourself is not

Apply Yourself is a document check. We do not search for properties, arrange viewings or contact landlords for you, and we cannot guarantee that a landlord accepts your application. We check that your documents are complete and consistent; we do not verify that they are authentic or approve you as a tenant.

FAQ

Which documents will I need?
It depends on your situation. Typically: ID or passport, proof of income such as payslips or an employment contract, recent bank statements, and a landlord reference or a guarantor. After you pay you choose your profile and see the exact list.
How fast is the review?
Within 2 working days of sending your documents for review.
Who checks my documents?
A person at HQ Housing, not software.
Can I cancel?
Checked dossier: yes, within 14 days of payment for a full refund, as long as you have not sent your documents for review. Templates pack: no, because you get access straight away. Cancellation details
What happens to my documents?
They are stored in the EU and only you and the HQ Housing reviewers can open them. We delete them 3 months after your dossier is delivered, and we email you a week before so you can download your dossier. If you never finish your file, we delete your documents 6 months after payment, again with an email a week before. Privacy policy
Is it available in other languages?
Apply Yourself is in English for now.
