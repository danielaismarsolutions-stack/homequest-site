**STEP 01 — Tell us what you're looking for**

**10 minutes**

Complete the purchase intake form and tell us what you're looking for.

We discuss:

- Budget 

- Preferred locations 

- Property type 

- Timeline 

- Financing requirements 

- Investment or owner-occupier plans 

The clearer the brief, the more focused the search.

**STEP 02 — Independent mortgage advice**

**Before activation**

Before we begin actively searching, we recommend that you obtain independent mortgage advice.

This allows us to determine:

- Your borrowing capacity 

- Expected monthly costs 

- Maximum purchase budget 

- Realistic buying opportunities 

HQ Housing does not provide mortgage advice.

Mortgage advice is always provided by an independent financial advisor.

**STEP 03 — Activation of the search**

**After positive mortgage assessment**

Once the mortgage advisor confirms that financing appears realistic, the search can be activated.

An Activation Deposit of **€750 excl. VAT** becomes payable at this stage.

The Activation Deposit is:

- Non-refundable 

- Fully deductible from the final brokerage fee 

- Intended to cover the initial work involved in launching the purchase search 

**STEP 04 — Property search & shortlist**

**Immediately after activation**

We search through:

- Public listings 

- Broker networks 

- Off-market opportunities 

- Existing industry contacts 

Suitable properties are presented together with our honest assessment of:

- Asking price 

- Market position 

- Risks 

- Negotiation opportunities 

**STEP 05 — Viewings & due diligence**

**As opportunities arise**

We assist with:

- Scheduling viewings 

- Attending viewings where possible 

- Identifying potential concerns 

- Discussing strengths and weaknesses 

- Reviewing available documentation 

Our objective is to help you make informed decisions.

**STEP 06 — Offer strategy & negotiations**

**When you've found the right property**

Once you decide to proceed:

- We prepare the offer 

- Advise on bidding strategy 

- Negotiate with the seller or selling agent 

- Coordinate communication throughout the process 

The goal is not simply to win the property, but to secure it under the best possible conditions.

**STEP 07 — Offer accepted**

**40% of brokerage fee becomes payable**

Once your offer has been formally accepted by the seller:

- The purchase process is secured 

- Financing and legal formalities continue 

- The first success milestone has been reached 

At this stage:

**40% of the agreed brokerage fee becomes due and payable.**

**STEP 08 — Financing & purchase agreement**

**Following acceptance**

We coordinate with:

- Mortgage advisors 

- Banks 

- Notaries 

- Selling agents 

We assist in keeping the transaction on track until all conditions have been satisfied.

**STEP 09 — Mortgage approved & completion scheduled**

**Final invoice**

Once:

- Financing has been approved; and 

- The transfer date has been confirmed; 

the remaining part of our fee becomes payable.

At this stage:

**60% of the agreed brokerage fee becomes due, less the €750 Activation Deposit already paid.**

**STEP 10 — Notary & key handover**

**Completion day**

We remain available through the final stage:

- Final inspection 

- Notary appointment 

- Transfer of ownership 

- Key handover 

The assignment concludes once ownership has successfully transferred.

**Fee Summary**

**Brokerage Fee:** 1.25% of the purchase price (excl. VAT)

**Payment structure**

**Stage 1**

- €750 Activation Deposit (excl. VAT) 

- Payable only after a positive mortgage assessment 

- Fully deductible from the final fee 

**Stage 2**

- 40% of the brokerage fee 

- Payable once the seller formally accepts the offer 

**Stage 3**

- Remaining 60% of the brokerage fee 

- Less the €750 Activation Deposit already paid 

- Payable once financing is approved and the transfer date has been confirmed
