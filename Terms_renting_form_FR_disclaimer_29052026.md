**HQ Housing – Rental Search Terms & Conditions**

At **HQ Housing** we are happy to help you find your ideal home. The following terms apply to all rental search services.

**Costs**

We charge a fee of one month's rent excluding 21% VAT.

If a property is rented for a single person or couple through the use of one or more guarantors, we charge a fee equal to one and a half (1.5) months' rent excluding 21% VAT.

In all cases, a minimum fee of €1,250 excluding 21% VAT applies.

The brokerage fee becomes due immediately upon the Client being offered a rental agreement, tenancy agreement, lease agreement or any other form of rental contract by the landlord, property owner, agent or housing provider following the introduction, mediation or involvement of HQ Housing.

The fee shall remain payable irrespective of whether the Client signs the offered agreement, delays signing, withdraws after receiving the agreement, or otherwise fails to complete the rental process.

The success fee shall also remain due where the Client rents, leases or otherwise secures a property introduced by HQ Housing, whether directly or indirectly, whether during or after the search assignment, and irrespective of whether the final agreement is concluded through HQ Housing, the landlord, a third party, a family member, a guarantor or any other intermediary.

**Withdrawal Costs**

If a rental proposal submitted on behalf of the Client is accepted by the landlord and the Client subsequently withdraws, the Client shall be liable for a withdrawal fee equal to 50% of the proposed monthly rent excluding VAT.

If the Client withdraws the proposal before a response has been received from the landlord, a fixed fee of €250 excluding VAT shall apply.
